More than a decade of modular building

Glorious Container Conversions Uganda Limited designs, fabricates and delivers container buildings from its yard in Bugolobi Industrial Area, Kampala.

The company

Fast, modular, cost-effective, eco-friendly

Our team brings more than a decade of expertise in converting shipping containers into infrastructure. The range runs from simple storage units to high-specification, bespoke conversions: homes, offices, clinics, laboratories, cold rooms, restaurants and ablution blocks.

Everything is built to the client's specification. Layouts, finishes and colours are agreed on CAD drawings before fabrication starts, and the drawings are with you within 48 hours of your first enquiry.

Because all conversion work happens at our own premises, a unit arrives on site finished. Our in-house transport division moves it by road anywhere in Uganda and to neighbouring countries, and our trained team erects and commissions it.

Mission

To meet our clients' needs for fast, modular, cost-effective and eco-friendly infrastructure using shipping containers and other modular systems.

Vision

To be the market leader in modular construction, using recycled shipping containers as the building blocks for society's infrastructure needs and green housing.

How we work

Five commitments

Steel first, then everything that makes it a building: insulation, lining, services, finishes.

  • 01

    Sustainability

    Every build gives a decommissioned container a second life. Solar, rainwater and insulation options cut running costs.

  • 02

    Customisation

    Layouts, openings, linings and colours follow your drawings, not a catalogue.

  • 03

    Durability

    Steel built for the ocean, finished with anti-vandal windows, proper insulation and protective coatings.

  • 04

    Efficient installation

    Finished off-site, placed by crane, commissioned by our own trained team.

  • 05

    Budget-conscious

    From a storage box to a bespoke clinic, scope and price are fixed before fabrication begins.
