How it works

From enquiry to keys

All conversion work happens at our own premises. Your unit arrives finished, is craned onto its base and commissioned by our team.

  1. Step 01

    Enquiry

    Tell us the use, the site and the budget. We advise on new or used units and the right size.

    Day 0
  2. Step 02

    Design

    CAD drawings of the proposed layout and elevations are delivered within 48 hours of the first enquiry.

    Within 48 hrs
  3. Step 03

    Fabrication

    Cutting, welding, insulation, lining, electrics and finishes at the Bugolobi yard, in your brand colours.

    Workshop build
  4. Step 04

    Delivery

    Road transport by our in-house division, crane placement, erection and commissioning on site.

    Anywhere by road

Why containers

Built for the sea. Ready for the site.

Corten steel, a rigid frame and standard dimensions mean every unit is strong, stackable and transportable before we have touched it.

Two-high stackingSite build

  • 01

    Fast

    Workshop fabrication runs in parallel with site preparation. Units arrive finished, not as materials.

  • 02

    Relocatable

    Move the building when you move the business. We relocate units as a standard part of the service.

  • 03

    Durable

    Designed to carry cargo across oceans, a container shrugs off weather, pests and rough handling.

  • 04

    Resource-efficient

    Every build reuses a decommissioned unit. Solar power, rainwater harvesting and efficient insulation are available options.

  • 05

    Budget-certain

    A fixed scope built off-site keeps costs predictable, from a single storage box to a high-spec bespoke conversion.
